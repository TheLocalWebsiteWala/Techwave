Our story

We started in a small studio with a simple frustration: most tech accessories were either beautiful or useful, rarely both. So we began designing the things we wanted to own — objects that feel considered in the hand and disappear into daily life.

Every product goes through months of prototyping before it earns a place in the range. If it doesn't improve on what already exists, we don't make it.
